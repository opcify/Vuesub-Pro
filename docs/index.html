Why Vuesub

Whisper accuracy

OpenAI's Whisper model running locally — multi-lingual, punctuation-aware, no upload.

FCP-style editor

Waveform timeline, draggable caption clips, inline text editing, multi-select merge.

Export anywhere

SRT, VTT, plain text, or FCPXML for Final Cut Pro at any frame rate.

Fully offline

No account, no upload, no telemetry. Your audio never leaves the machine.

System requirements

macOS 11 Big Sur or later. Apple Silicon or Intel. About 1 GB free disk for the app + a Whisper model. Models are downloaded on first use; you can pick anywhere from tiny (75 MB) to large-v3 (3 GB) depending on accuracy/speed tradeoff you want.
